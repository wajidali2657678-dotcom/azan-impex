From The Factory

Notes on sublimation printing.

What makes sublimation different?

Unlike screen printing, sublimation turns dye into a gas that bonds directly into the fabric fibers — so the design becomes part of the fabric itself, not a layer sitting on top. That's why it doesn't crack or peel with washing.

Why polyester works best

Sublimation dye bonds most effectively with polyester and polyester-blend fabrics, which is why most sports jerseys and performance wear use it — it holds vivid color while staying lightweight and breathable.

Planning your first order

Most buyers start with a clear design file and a target quantity. Sharing both upfront — along with size breakdown — helps get an accurate quote faster.

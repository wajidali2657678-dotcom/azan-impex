Factory Floor Workflow

Our Production Process

Complete transparency from the moment you submit your tech pack to the day your order is delivered.

01

Design Setup

We review your tech packs, logos, and specific requirements to generate production-ready vector artworks.

02

Sublimation Printing

Using imported Italian inks and high-speed plotters, we print your designs onto transfer paper with perfect color matching.

03

Heat Transfer

High-pressure industrial heat presses vaporize the ink directly into the polyester fibers for permanent, unfading colors.

04

Cutting & Stitching

Precision laser cutting ensures accurate sizing, followed by reinforced sewing by our experienced stitching craftsmen.

05

Quality Control

Every garment undergoes strict QC to check sizing dimensions, stitching integrity, and color consistency before packing.

06

Global Delivery

Securely poly-bagged and boxed. We dispatch via reliable air freight (DHL/FedEx) direct to your doorstep worldwide.
